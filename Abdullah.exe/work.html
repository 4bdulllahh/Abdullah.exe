HomingPigeon

Safe, compliant cold email for small businesses

The problem

Small businesses burn their domain reputation on outreach they don't understand, and end up permanently in spam folders.

What I built

A cross-platform desktop app that checks your SPF, DKIM and DMARC setup before you send a single message, then ramps volume gradually from ~20 a day with randomised delays, office-hours scheduling and holiday awareness. It scores messages for spam signals before they go, validates contacts on import, reads the inbox for bounces, handles unsubscribes with proper List-Unsubscribe headers and halts automatically if failures spike. Everything stays on the user's machine, with credentials encrypted through the OS keystore.

Currently in public beta — my most-starred repository.

Python PyQt6 SQLite Multi-threading SMTP/IMAP DNS

Face-Tracking Laser Turret

Computer vision driving physical hardware

The problem

I wanted to find out where software stops and the physical world starts.

What I built

A 3D-printed two-axis turret driven by two servos. A Python process reads the laptop camera, detects faces in real time with OpenCV, converts their position into servo angles and streams those over serial to an Arduino running the motor control firmware. The repository holds the CAD files, the firmware, the vision code and the wiring diagrams.
